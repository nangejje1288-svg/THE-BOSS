03The story beneath the milestones

The narrative arc

From a secure start
to a future of her own.

Her life story is more than a timeline. The central thread is how a young woman uses the opportunities and support around her while discovering what she herself wants to build. Her family background is part of that context—not a substitute for her own effort, personality or choices.

The book can move between summary time—the broader years, routines and changes—and scenic time: a particular school morning, a conversation that stayed with her, or a small turning point she remembers clearly. Those scenes should come from her, not be invented to make the story sound dramatic.

Narration choice: the finished memoir can speak as “I” or describe Sharine as “she.” Whichever is chosen, her voice, verified details and boundaries come first.
